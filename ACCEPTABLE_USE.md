# RunPublic acceptable use policy

Effective 21 August 2026.

RunPublic is a public-beta developer tunnel. You are responsible for every
service and response made available through your account.

You may not use RunPublic to:

- break the law, infringe rights, or access systems without authorization;
- distribute malware, phishing pages, credential-harvesting forms, spam, or
  deceptive content;
- expose databases, internal admin panels, secrets, or sensitive personal,
  health, financial, or authentication data without appropriate legal basis and
  application-level safeguards;
- evade platform limits, probe other tenants, disrupt the service, or create
  excessive automated traffic that harms other users;
- impersonate another person or organization, or use a hostname in a misleading
  way; or
- resell the hosted beta or use it as permanent production hosting without
  written permission.

Tunnel URLs are public by design. RunPublic may rate-limit, suspend, or remove
accounts and hostnames to protect users, the service, or third parties. The
public beta has no uptime guarantee.

Report a security-sensitive violation through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting for `hey-edison/runpublic`. Use a normal GitHub issue only when the
report contains no credentials, personal data, or private URLs.
